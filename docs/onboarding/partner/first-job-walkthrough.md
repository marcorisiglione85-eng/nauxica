## Purpose

This document walks you through your first job request on the Nauxica platform — from the moment the alert arrives to the moment the job is marked complete and your invoice is sent.

Every subsequent job follows the same pattern. After your first, you will not need this document. Read it before the first one.

---

## Before Any Job Arrives — Be Ready

Before your first job request lands, verify these are in order.

| Check | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| SMS notifications active on your phone | Job requests arrive by SMS as well as in-platform. You may not be watching the app. |
| Platform notifications enabled on your device | In-platform push notifications, if installed. |
| Response window understood for your service type | Missing the window means the request moves to the next partner. Check it now. |
| Profile photo and description live | Homeowners can see your profile after assignment — first impressions count before the first job. |

**Response windows:**

| Your service type | You have this long to respond |
|---|---|
| Cleaning | 2 hours |
| Maintenance (routine) | 4 hours |
| Maintenance (urgent) | 30 minutes |
| Transfer | 1 hour |
| Experience | 4 hours |
| Pool / Garden | 24 hours |

The window starts the moment the request is sent — not the moment you first see it. Keep notifications active.

## Stage 1 — The Job Request Arrives

### 1.1 What you receive

When a homeowner creates a service request and you are the assigned partner, you receive:

- **SMS alert** to your registered mobile: short message with job type, date/time, and a link to the platform
- **In-platform notification** with the same information

### 1.2 What the request shows at this stage

Before you accept, you see:

| Information shown | What you see |
|---|---|
| Service type | e.g. CLEANING / MAINTENANCE URGENT |
| Property area | Municipality and neighbourhood — not the full address |
| Property type | e.g. Two-bedroom apartment, villa with pool |
| Date and time required | Specific date/time window or "as soon as possible" |
| Brief description | 1–2 sentences about the job (e.g. "Post-checkout deep clean — 2 bedrooms, 1 bathroom") |
| Homeowner contact | Not yet visible |
| Full address | Not yet visible — revealed at acceptance |
| Access codes | Not yet visible — revealed at acceptance |

**You are deciding to accept based on: service type, area, property type, and date/time.** This is enough to judge whether you can take the job. The full property details come after you commit.

---

## Stage 2 — Accept or Decline

### 2.1 Accepting the request

If you can take the job:

1. Open the request in the platform (link in SMS or in-platform notification)
2. Review the summary
3. Tap or click **Accept**
4. Your acceptance is recorded immediately

**At acceptance, the system:**
- Marks the job as `accepted` in your name
- Notifies the homeowner that you have accepted
- Reveals the full property address
- Sends your access code(s) — key box combination, partner entry code, or key safe details
- Makes the full Partner Brief available for this job

These are sent via in-platform message and/or push notification. Check both.

### 2.2 Declining the request

If you cannot take the job, tap **Decline** and select a reason:

| Decline reason | When to use |
|---|---|
| Date/time conflict | You have another confirmed commitment |
| Outside my coverage area | The property is further than you can reliably reach |
| Unavailable this period | You are on leave or have temporarily paused availability |
| Job scope too large | The job description exceeds what you can complete alone |

Declining is legitimate. Partners decline for valid reasons. **What matters is that you respond within the window** — accepting or declining. Failing to respond (no reply at all) is what damages your standing. A genuine decline does not.

When you decline, the request moves automatically to the next assigned partner in priority order. If you are the only assigned partner for this property and service type, the homeowner and Nauxica are notified directly.

### 2.3 The urgency of the response window

If you are assigned as a preferred partner, the homeowner is counting on you specifically. Every minute you delay in responding is a minute the homeowner does not know whether their service need is covered.

For MAINTENANCE (urgent) requests: **you have 30 minutes.** If you see the alert and cannot respond within the window, decline immediately so the next partner is contacted without delay. A fast decline is more professional than a slow one.

## Stage 3 — Before You Travel

### 3.1 Read the Partner Brief

After acceptance, open the **Partner Brief** in the job detail view. This is a curated summary of the property prepared for your service type. It contains:

**For all service types:**
- Full property address
- Property type and capacity
- Access method and your specific access code
- Entry instructions (step-by-step)
- Key return instructions after the job
- Emergency contact: the homeowner's name and phone number (for you to use if you find a serious problem)
- Any operational notes from your assignment

**For Cleaning jobs, additionally:**
- Cleaning inventory and supplies notes
- Linen changeover instructions
- Any property quirks (fragile items, special surfaces, appliances that need care)
- What the departing guest was expected to have done (e.g. dishes done, bins emptied)
- Checkout tasks completed vs. still needed

**For Maintenance jobs, additionally:**
- Specific issue description from the service request
- Gas, water, and electricity shutoff locations
- Any known property quirks relevant to your trade
- Maintenance priority items (if any pre-existing conditions)

**For Transfers:**
- Pickup address and dropoff address
- Guest name and number of passengers
- Flight or ferry reference (if provided by homeowner)
- Any guest-specific notes (e.g. luggage quantity, mobility requirements)

Read the Partner Brief before you travel. Do not arrive at the property and discover the access code was in the brief you did not read.

### 3.2 Verify the access code before you travel

The access code is in the job brief. Before you leave for the property:
- Note the code
- Note the access method (key box type, smart lock, key safe)
- Note the exact location of the access point (this is in the entry instructions)

**Do not assume you will remember the code from the notification.** Write it down or keep the job open on your phone.

### 3.3 If you cannot see the access code

If the access code field shows `[not yet available]` or is blank:
- Do not travel to the property — you will not be able to enter
- Contact the homeowner using the emergency contact in the Partner Brief
- If you cannot reach the homeowner, contact the Nauxica team at [contact method]

This is a data issue. Do not attempt to access the property by other means.

## Stage 4 — Arrival and Property Access

### 4.1 Follow the entry instructions

The entry instructions in the Partner Brief are written specifically for partners. Follow them exactly:

- The instructions include the access point location, the door/gate sequence, and any building-specific steps
- If the instructions say "second door on the left after the main gate" — confirm the door before entering
- If the property has multiple entry points, the instructions specify which one to use for partner access

### 4.2 If the access code does not work

First check:
- [ ] Are you at the right entry point? (The instructions specify which door/gate)
- [ ] Is the code correct? (Re-read from the brief — do not rely on memory)
- [ ] Is the code entry sequence correct? (Some boxes require Enter/Confirm after the code)

If the code genuinely does not work after these checks:
1. Call the homeowner using the emergency contact in the Partner Brief
2. If no answer: message via the in-platform job chat
3. If no response within 15 minutes for urgent jobs: contact the Nauxica team

Do not ask a neighbour for access. Do not attempt to contact guests if any are present. Do not force any entry point.

### 4.3 If you find the property in unexpected condition

Before beginning work, do a quick visual check:
- Is there obvious pre-existing damage you did not cause?
- Is there anything that would be unsafe to proceed with? (e.g. gas smell, flooding, electrical hazard)
- Is the property in a state significantly different from what the job brief described?

If yes: **stop and report before beginning work.** See Stage 6 — Reporting Issues.

## Stage 5 — Completing the Job

### 5.1 Complete what you were asked to do

Your job scope is defined in the service request. Do not expand it beyond what was requested without homeowner confirmation.

| If you are asked to... | Do |
|---|---|
| Clean a 2-bedroom apartment post-checkout | Complete the checkout clean as specified |
| Fix a specific maintenance issue | Fix that issue and document what you did |
| Transfer 2 passengers from Catania airport | Transfer those passengers |

If additional work is clearly needed (e.g. you're cleaning and you find a broken tap), do not fix it yourself unless you are also assigned for maintenance and the homeowner explicitly asks. **Report it.** See Stage 6.

### 5.2 Take completion photos

For Cleaning and Maintenance jobs, photos are required at job completion. This protects you as much as the homeowner.

**Cleaning — required photos:**
- Each bedroom: made beds, tidy surfaces
- Each bathroom: cleaned surfaces, toilet, fresh towels if applicable
- Kitchen: clean worktops, empty sink, hob
- Living area: tidy, cushions straight
- Any outdoor area in scope: terrace, pool surround

**Maintenance — required photos:**
- Before: the problem as found
- During (if relevant): any access panels opened, parts removed
- After: the completed fix in context
- Materials used (if applicable)

Poor or missing completion photos are the most common cause of disputes. Take them before you leave. The homeowner cannot dispute a job that is well-documented.

### 5.3 Service-specific notes

**Cleaning:**
- Respect the departure inventory. If items are missing or damaged that you didn't cause, report them — see Stage 6
- Do not open locked storage areas unless the brief specifically instructs it
- Leave the property secured — all windows and doors closed and locked before exit
- Do not leave personal items behind

**Maintenance:**
- If you need to order parts or return for a second visit, communicate this via the in-platform job chat — do not commit to a date with the homeowner privately
- Leave the property clean and tidy around your work area
- If you opened any utility panel, return it to its original position and confirm it is secure
- Test any fixed component before leaving — if it fails on your test, it will fail on the homeowner's

**Transfers:**
- Greet guests with the passenger name as confirmed in the brief (not the homeowner's name)
- Do not share homeowner contact details with guests
- Do not discuss property details, rental terms, or pricing with guests
- If a guest asks about something you cannot help with, direct them to the Nauxica concierge: "You can ask the Nauxica concierge on WhatsApp — they'll be able to help with that"

## Stage 6 — Reporting Issues

This is the most important stage to understand before your first job. **Never handle a property issue informally.** Every issue must go through the platform — this protects you legally and gives the homeowner a record.

### 6.1 What to report

| Issue type | Report via |
|---|---|
| Pre-existing damage you found (not caused by you) | In-platform job report — describe and photograph before touching |
| Damage you caused during the job | In-platform job report — immediate report, do not hide |
| Guest left property in significantly worse condition than expected | In-platform job report |
| Maintenance issue discovered during a cleaning job | In-platform job report — do not attempt repair unless also assigned for maintenance |
| Safety hazard found | In-platform urgent flag — this triggers immediate homeowner notification |
| Gas smell or possible leak | Leave property immediately. Call 112 (emergency) then homeowner. Report in platform. |
| Signs of flooding or water damage | Document, notify homeowner immediately via emergency contact, report in platform |
| Item you suspect is stolen | Report in platform — do not make accusations verbally |
| Any access code that did not work | Report in platform for the homeowner to update |

### 6.2 How to report in the platform

1. Open the job in your dashboard
2. Navigate to **Report an issue**
3. Select the issue category
4. Describe clearly: what you found, where, when
5. Upload photos
6. Submit — the homeowner and Nauxica are notified

**Do not rely on verbal communication with the homeowner to resolve property issues.** Platform records are the only records that count in a dispute.

### 6.3 What you must NOT do

- Do not fix pre-existing damage without authorisation (unless it is an immediate safety risk)
- Do not dispose of guest belongings you find at the property
- Do not use homeowner supplies beyond what the job requires
- Do not share what you find at the property with anyone outside the platform
- Do not photograph or share personal items belonging to guests or homeowners

## Stage 7 — Completing and Exiting

### 7.1 Before you leave

- [ ] Job scope completed as requested
- [ ] Completion photos taken
- [ ] Any issues reported in the platform
- [ ] All access points secured — doors locked, windows closed
- [ ] Key returned to the specified location (the Partner Brief specifies where)
- [ ] No personal tools or materials left behind
- [ ] Property in the condition described in the job scope

### 7.2 Key return

The key return location and method is in your Partner Brief. Return the key exactly as specified:
- Key box: replace the key, close and lock the box, confirm the lock is engaged
- Key safe: follow the specific return sequence
- In-person handover: confirm handover via in-platform message

**Do not retain access codes or physical keys after job completion.** Access credentials expire and are rotated. A code that worked today may not work tomorrow.

### 7.3 Mark the job as complete

In the platform:
1. Open the job in your dashboard
2. Tap **Mark as complete**
3. Confirm that completion photos are attached
4. Add a brief completion note (optional but recommended for first jobs — e.g. "2-bed clean completed, fresh linen on both beds, all surfaces clean, spare key returned to box")
5. Submit

The homeowner is notified that the job is complete. The job status changes to `completed`.

## Stage 8 — After the Job

### 8.1 The homeowner review

After you mark the job complete, the homeowner is invited to leave a rating and review. Ratings are 1–5 stars with an optional written comment.

The homeowner's first impression of your work is formed now — from your completion note, your photos, and the condition they observe when they or the next guest arrive.

**Your rating matters.** It is the primary signal homeowners use when deciding whether to keep you as a preferred partner or to assign you to additional properties. It is also tracked by Nauxica for platform monitoring purposes.

### 8.2 Invoicing the homeowner

For direct-service jobs (Cleaning, Maintenance, Laundry, Pool/Garden):
- You invoice the homeowner directly using your own invoicing process
- Include the Nauxica job reference number on your invoice
- Invoice currency: EUR
- Payment terms: as agreed with the homeowner (platform does not enforce payment terms for direct jobs)

For platform-processed jobs (Experiences, Transfers booked via AI concierge):
- Nauxica processes the guest payment and remits to you
- Platform commission is deducted per your Partner Agreement
- Payment timeline: as specified in your Partner Agreement

### 8.3 If the homeowner disputes the job

If a homeowner raises a dispute about the quality or completion of your job, you will be notified via the platform. Respond promptly and specifically — the dispute record includes your completion photos and job notes.

See [dispute-resolution.md](../../trust-safety/dispute-resolution.md) for the full dispute process.

A well-documented job (photos, completion note, issue reports) is almost always resolvable. An undocumented one is much harder to defend.

## Common First-Job Issues

| Issue | What happened | How to handle |
|---|---|---|
| Access code doesn't work | Code was changed or entry instructions not followed precisely | Re-read entry instructions; try sequence steps again; contact homeowner via emergency contact if still failing |
| Property is much dirtier than expected (cleaning) | Guest departure condition worse than standard | Do not refuse the job on-site — complete what you can within your agreed time; report the excess condition via the platform; contact homeowner if scope is significantly beyond what was booked |
| You find existing damage before starting (maintenance) | Pre-existing issue | Photograph and report before touching anything — this protects you from being held responsible |
| The job takes longer than expected | Scope larger than described | Contact homeowner via in-platform message immediately; do not walk off mid-job without communication |
| Guest is still at the property during a cleaning or maintenance job | Checkout delayed or homeowner did not confirm checkout | Contact homeowner immediately. Do not enter an occupied property without the homeowner's explicit instruction. |
| You cannot safely complete the maintenance job (beyond your skills) | Issue is more complex than described | Do not attempt work you are not qualified for. Report via platform immediately with description. |
| Guest asks you questions about the property | Transfer or experience partner; guest is curious | Answer only what is directly relevant to your service. For any platform or property questions: "The Nauxica concierge on WhatsApp will be able to help you with that." |
| You accidentally damage something | Accidents happen | Report immediately via the platform. Do not hide it. Immediate, honest reporting is always better than the homeowner discovering it later. |
| You cannot find the key return location | Brief instructions unclear | Contact homeowner via emergency contact. Do not leave the property unsecured or retain the key. |

## Stage 9 — Emergency Escalation

Emergency escalation is separate from issue reporting. Use this section for any situation that involves a risk to physical safety — not just a difficult job.

### 9.1 Rule: safety first, platform second

If you encounter an emergency at a property, your first priority is the safety of anyone present — including yourself. Call emergency services immediately. Do not wait to open the platform, do not attempt to photograph the situation first, do not wait for homeowner instructions.

Platform notification comes after safety is addressed.

### 9.2 Italian emergency numbers

| Emergency | Number |
|---|---|
| All emergencies (single line) | **112** |
| Police | 113 |
| Fire brigade | 115 |
| Medical / ambulance | 118 |
| Coast guard | 1530 |

For any situation involving a potential threat to life or property safety, call **112** if uncertain which service is needed.

### 9.3 Emergency response by situation

| Situation | Your immediate action |
|---|---|
| Person requiring medical assistance | Call 118. Keep the person calm and still unless in immediate physical danger. Do not move them if injured. Stay on the line with the operator. |
| Fire or smoke | Evacuate all people immediately. Do not take belongings. Call 115. Do not re-enter under any circumstances. |
| Gas smell | Do not use any electrical switches (including light switches). Do not call from inside the property. Evacuate immediately. Call 112 from outside. |
| Flooding or burst pipe | Locate the water shutoff if specified in the Partner Brief and if safe to reach. Call 112 if structural damage is visible or suspected. Notify homeowner. |
| Security threat or intruder | Remove yourself from the property. Call 113. Do not confront. |
| Structural concern (cracks, collapse sounds) | Evacuate immediately. Call 112. Do not re-enter until cleared by emergency services. |

### 9.4 Notifying Nauxica after an emergency

After calling emergency services and ensuring safety:

1. Open the job in the platform
2. Use **Report emergency** in the job record
3. Record: what you found, what you did, which services were called, your current status
4. The Nauxica founder is notified immediately via emergency escalation

Do not wait until the job is closed or the situation has resolved to submit the emergency report. Report as soon as it is safe to do so.

## Quality Scoring

### How your partner record is built

Every job you complete is recorded against your platform profile. The platform tracks five metrics:

| Metric | What it measures |
|---|---|
| **Response rate** | Percentage of job requests you responded to within the response window |
| **Acceptance rate** | Percentage of your responses that were acceptances |
| **Completion rate** | Percentage of accepted jobs marked as complete with required evidence |
| **Average homeowner rating** | Star rating (1–5) from homeowner post-job reviews |
| **Dispute rate** | Number of disputes opened against you in any rolling 90-day window |

### How homeowners rate jobs

After you mark a job complete, the homeowner is prompted to rate it on a 1–5 scale with an optional written comment. Rating is optional — not all homeowners complete it — but those who do create a visible record on your marketplace profile.

Rating visibility:
- Your aggregate star rating is visible on your public marketplace profile
- Individual ratings are visible to you in **Dashboard → Performance**
- Ratings are visible to homeowners browsing the marketplace

### Quality thresholds and monitoring

At MVP, there are no hard performance cut-offs that trigger automatic action. Performance is monitored manually by the Nauxica founder. As volume grows, the following thresholds inform risk classification:

| Threshold | Indicator |
|---|---|
| Response rate below 80% | Reliability concern |
| Acceptance rate below 60% | Coverage area or availability may be misconfigured |
| Average rating below 3.5 (after 10+ jobs) | Quality review required |
| 2+ disputes in any 90-day period | Pattern review |
| Insurance expiry with no renewal | Immediate flag |

These thresholds do not automatically suspend your account. They trigger a review conversation with the Nauxica team.

## Suspension Triggers

### Monitoring flags (trigger review, not immediate suspension)

| Trigger | Threshold |
|---|---|
| Non-response rate | 3 non-responses in any 90-day period |
| Average homeowner rating | Below 3.5 after 10 or more completed jobs |
| Active dispute count | 2 or more D-05, D-06, or D-07 disputes in any 90-day period |
| Insurance expiry | Certificate reaches expiry with no renewal uploaded |

A monitoring flag means the Nauxica team contacts you to understand what has happened. It is not a suspension and does not affect your current job queue.

### Immediate suspension triggers

The following result in immediate account suspension before any review:

| Trigger | Reason |
|---|---|
| Insurance lapses without renewal | Active partner without required coverage is a platform risk |
| Solicitation of homeowner or guest outside platform | Direct violation of the Partner Agreement |
| Fraudulent completion record | Marking a job complete without attending |
| Confirmed property damage attributable to partner conduct | Safety and liability |
| Confirmed safety incident attributable to partner conduct | Guest or property harm |
| Criminal conviction affecting service eligibility | Fitness for role — applies to Tier 2 partners particularly |

If your account is suspended:
- You receive a written notification with the specific reason
- Pending job requests are reassigned
- Your marketplace profile is hidden to homeowners
- You may appeal within 7 days of the suspension notice

Reinstatement after a first suspension is possible if the underlying issue is resolved. Third suspension is permanent removal. Full process: [partner-vetting.md](../../trust-safety/partner-vetting.md)

## After Your First Job — What Changes

Your first completed job establishes your standing on the platform:

- Your job record is now active — homeowners can see your completion count
- If the homeowner rates you, your rating is visible (once you have 3+ reviews)
- You are eligible for the homeowner to assign you to additional properties or additional service types
- The Nauxica team checks first-job records to confirm the platform is operating correctly

One job well done is the foundation of a long-term preferred partner relationship. Homeowners who find a reliable partner do not look for another one.
