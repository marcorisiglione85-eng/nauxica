## Overview

This guide walks a new service partner through the complete process of joining Nauxica — from initial registration to receiving your first job request.

Nauxica is a property management platform serving short-term rental homeowners in Sicily. Partners are the operational layer that delivers cleaning, maintenance, transfers, laundry, and experiences to the properties and guests managed through the platform. Homeowners select and assign partners from the vetted marketplace. When a service is needed, Nauxica dispatches a job request to your preferred partners in priority order.

**You do not work for Nauxica.** You are an independent service provider. Your contract is with the homeowner. Nauxica provides the platform, the booking flow, and the job request infrastructure. You invoice the homeowner directly for your services.

**Estimated time to activation:** 1–2 hours for Tier 1 (Transfers, Experiences, Laundry). 3–5 working days for Tier 2 (Cleaning, Maintenance) — most of this is document review time, not active work on your part.

**MVP model:** Every application is reviewed by the Nauxica founder before activation. No automated approvals. This ensures that the first cohort of partners represents genuine platform quality.

## Who This Guide Is For

- Independent cleaners, cleaning companies, and housekeeping services operating in Sicily

- Local experience providers (tour guides, boat trip operators, cooking class hosts, wine tasting specialists)

One partner account can cover multiple service types if you offer more than one.

## Step 1 — Account Registration

**Platform:** Registration page at [FOUNDER_DECISION_REQUIRED]
**Estimated time:** 10 minutes

### 1.1 Create your account

1. Navigate to the Nauxica registration page
2. Select account type: **Service Partner**
3. Enter: email address, password, full legal name (or company name), mobile phone number
4. Accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy
5. Click **Create account**

### 1.2 Verify your email

- A verification email is sent immediately
- Click the link in the email — it expires after 24 hours
- Check spam if it does not arrive within 5 minutes

### 1.3 Verify your phone number

- An SMS OTP is sent to your mobile number
- Enter the 6-digit code in the platform
- This number receives job request alerts — keep it active and reachable

### 1.4 Enter your tax identity

- Enter your **codice fiscale** (sole traders and individuals) or **Partita IVA** (businesses)
- This is stored encrypted and used for identity verification and invoicing purposes
- It is not visible to homeowners

**Blocker:** If you do not complete email and phone verification within 72 hours, the account is deactivated. Re-register to restart.

## Step 2 — Service Type and Operating Area

**Estimated time:** 5–10 minutes

### 2.1 Select your service type(s)

Select every service type you offer. You can add or remove service types later — but any change to a Tier 2 service type (Cleaning, Maintenance) will trigger a document re-check.

| Service type code | What it covers |
|---|---|
| `CLEANING` | Post-checkout deep clean, mid-stay refresh, emergency clean |
| `MAINTENANCE` | Repairs, technical fixes, installations; at MVP also covers pool and garden maintenance |
| `LAUNDRY` | Linen and towel changeover, washing, pressing |
| `TRANSFERS` | Airport/port pickups and dropoffs |
| `EXPERIENCES` | Tours, boat trips, cooking classes, wine tastings |

### 2.2 Define your operating area

1. Select the Sicilian provinces you cover: CT, ME, PA, SR, RG, CL, EN, AG, TP
2. Within each province, optionally specify municipalities (comuni) if your coverage is limited to specific towns
3. If your coverage is dynamic, mark your base location — homeowners will see your approximate area

**Tip:** Be specific about where you can and cannot reliably serve. A partner who accepts a job and then declines because of travel distance creates a serious operational problem for the homeowner. Be honest at this step — you can update your area later.

### 2.3 Typical availability

Provide a general indication of when you are available. This is not a binding calendar — it helps homeowners assess fit when selecting partners.

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| Typical working days | e.g. Monday–Saturday |
| Typical working hours | e.g. 07:00–18:00 |
| Advance notice needed | How much notice you need for a job — e.g. "24 hours for cleaning", "Same-day for urgent maintenance" |
| Seasonal availability | If you do not operate year-round, indicate your operating window |

## Step 3 — Document Submission

**Estimated time:** 15–30 minutes (upload time only)

### 3.1 Upload required documents

Navigate to **Profile → Documents** and upload each required item for your service type.

| Document | Format | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Government-issued ID | PDF or JPG/PNG — minimum 200 DPI | Both sides if national ID card |
| Codice fiscale or P.IVA card | PDF or JPG/PNG | |
| Insurance certificate(s) | PDF preferred | Must show policy number, coverage amount, named insured, expiry date |
| Criminal record certificate | PDF | Self-requested from Casellario Giudiziale — dated within 3 months |
| Vehicle documents (transfers) | PDF | Separate file per vehicle if covering multiple vehicles |
| Tourism/food licence (if applicable) | PDF | |

### 3.2 Document quality

Common reasons for document rejection:
- Image too blurry or dark to read policy details
- Insurance certificate does not clearly state coverage amount
- Criminal record certificate is more than 3 months old
- Name on insurance certificate does not match name on ID

Check each document before uploading: read the key fields on screen. If you cannot read the policy number and coverage amount, the reviewer cannot either.

### 3.3 Documents not yet available

If a document is pending (e.g. insurance renewal in progress, criminal record certificate requested but not yet received):
- Upload what you have and add a note in the document field
- Notify the Nauxica team via [FOUNDER_DECISION_REQUIRED] with an expected date
- Your application will remain `pending` — it cannot proceed to review until all required documents are present

## Step 4 — Profile Completion

**Estimated time:** 15–20 minutes

Your partner profile is visible to homeowners when they browse the marketplace. This is your first impression — invest in it.

### 4.1 Display name

What homeowners see on the marketplace. Options:
- Your full name (sole trader): e.g. "Giovanni Ferraro"
- Your business name: e.g. "Ferraro Cleaning Catania"
- Short, professional, and specific to your area

### 4.2 Profile photo

- A clear, professional headshot or company logo
- Minimum 400×400 pixels, well-lit
- For cleaning/maintenance services: a photo of you in work context (uniform, tools) is more convincing than a formal portrait

### 4.3 Service description

Write 3–5 sentences. Include:
- What service(s) you provide
- Your experience level (years in the field, number of properties served)
- Your operating area in Sicily
- Any specialisation (e.g. villa-grade cleaning, smart lock installation, boat transfers)

This is written by you and reviewed by Nauxica. Nauxica may suggest improvements before activation, but will not rewrite it without your knowledge.

**Write in Italian and English if possible.** Homeowners are a mix of Italian and international. A bilingual description reaches both.

### 4.4 Pricing information

Enter indicative pricing for each service type you offer:

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| Hourly rate | For time-based services (maintenance, cleaning) |
| Fixed price per job | If you price by job type (e.g. standard apartment clean: €X) |
| Per-person rate | For experiences or transfers |
| Minimum call-out | e.g. "Minimum 2 hours for maintenance" |

**Pricing is indicative, not binding.** Final pricing for each job is agreed between you and the homeowner. This information helps homeowners filter partners by expected cost range.

### 4.5 Languages spoken

Select languages you are comfortable working in for job communications. At MVP, platform communications are in Italian and English.

## Step 5 — Platform Agreement

**Estimated time:** 10–15 minutes to read; 2 minutes to sign

### 5.1 Read the Partner Agreement

Before signing, read [partner-agreement.md](../../legal/partner-agreement.md) in full. It covers:
- Your obligations as a service partner
- Payment terms and invoicing (you invoice the homeowner directly)
- How disputes are handled
- What Nauxica can and cannot be held responsible for
- Your data obligations under GDPR
- The 12% platform commission structure for experiences and transfers booked through the AI concierge

### 5.2 Sign digitally

The agreement is signed via a digital signature flow in the platform. You must be the account holder to sign.

**The agreement cannot be signed on behalf of someone else.** If you are registering a company account, the signatory must be an authorised representative of the company.

### 5.3 What you are agreeing to

Key obligations on your part:
- Respond to job requests within the response window for your service type (see below)
- Complete jobs to the standard described in the job request
- Not solicit homeowners or guests outside the platform for repeat business (see partner agreement for details)
- Maintain the required insurance coverage throughout your active period
- Report any issues found at a property (damage, safety concerns) to the homeowner via the platform

You are not agreeing to:
- An exclusive relationship — you can work with other clients outside Nauxica
- Minimum job volume commitments
- Being an employee of Nauxica

## Step 6 — Application Submission and Review

**Estimated time:** 0 active time. Wait for review.

### 6.1 Submit for review

When all steps are complete:
1. Navigate to **Profile → Review status**
2. The platform performs a completeness check — all required documents present, profile sections filled
3. If complete: click **Submit application for review**
4. Your account status changes to `pending_review`

If incomplete: the platform lists exactly what is missing. Complete those items and resubmit.

### 6.2 What happens during review

The Nauxica founder (or a designated team member) reviews your application against the criteria in [partner-vetting.md](../../trust-safety/partner-vetting.md):

- Identity verified against ID document
- Tax ID confirmed present and matching
- Documents reviewed for validity (coverage amounts, expiry dates, named insured)
- Service description reviewed for coherence and specificity
- Profile photo reviewed
- For Tier 2: criminal record certificate reviewed; references contacted if provided

**Review timelines:**
- Tier 1 (Transfers, Experiences, Laundry): 24–48 hours from submission
- Tier 2 (Cleaning, Maintenance): 3–5 working days from submission

### 6.3 Communication during review

You may receive a message from the Nauxica team during review if:
- A document needs clarification or re-upload
- The insurance certificate does not clearly state required details
- The service description needs expansion

Respond promptly — delays in your response extend the review period.

### 6.4 Outcome

**Approved:** You receive an approval notification. Your account status changes to `active`. Proceed to Step 7.

**Rejected:** You receive a rejection notification with the specific reason(s). The notification explains whether reapplication is permitted and under what conditions. See [partner-vetting.md](../../trust-safety/partner-vetting.md) for rejection criteria and the appeals process.

**Conditionally approved (founder discretion):** You may be activated with a note that a specific document is to be provided within X days (e.g. insurance renewal imminent). This is at founder discretion and is not guaranteed.

## Step 7 — Activation and Founder Onboarding Touch

**Estimated time:** 15–20 minutes

### 7.1 Activation confirmation

When approved, you receive:
- Account activation confirmation email
- Link to your live partner profile on the marketplace (visible to homeowners)
- Platform dashboard access with full partner features enabled

### 7.2 MVP activation call (optional but recommended)

During MVP, the founder may offer a short activation call for Tier 2 partners. This is not mandatory — but partners who take it tend to have a smoother first job. The call covers:
- How job requests work and what to expect
- How the Partner Brief is structured
- How to report issues found during a job
- Questions you have about the platform

Duration: approximately 15 minutes.

### 7.3 You are now in the marketplace

Homeowners can now see your profile, view your services and pricing, and assign you to their properties as a preferred partner.

**You will not receive job requests immediately.** Job requests only arrive when:
1. A homeowner has assigned you to a property
2. A service request is created for that property (e.g. checkout triggers a cleaning request)
3. You are the highest-priority active partner for that service type at that property

## Step 8 — Understanding Partner Assignments

You do not browse and bid on jobs. Homeowners select you as a preferred partner for their property and service type. When a need arises, you receive a job request automatically.

### 8.1 How assignments work

1. A homeowner finds your profile in the marketplace
2. They assign you to their property for a specific service type (e.g. cleaning)
3. You receive a notification that you have been assigned
4. You receive a brief summary of the property — not the full address, but the area and property type
5. You accept or decline the assignment. Declining does not affect your platform standing — but consistently declining assignments from the same area suggests your coverage area is misset.

See [partner-assignment-model.md](../../architecture/partner-assignment-model.md) for the full assignment lifecycle.

### 8.2 How job requests work

When a service is needed (e.g. a checkout triggers a cleaning request):

1. A `PartnerRequest` is sent to your account
2. You receive a notification (in-platform + SMS)
3. The request shows: property area, job type, date and time, brief description
4. You have a set window to respond before the system tries the next partner in priority order

**Response windows by service type:**

| Service type | Response window |
|---|---|
| CLEANING | 2 hours |
| MAINTENANCE (routine) | 4 hours |
| MAINTENANCE (urgent) | 30 minutes |
| TRANSFERS | 1 hour |
| EXPERIENCES | 4 hours |

Missing your response window is noted. Three no-responses in a 90-day period trigger an automatic flag for review. See [partner-vetting.md](../../trust-safety/partner-vetting.md) for monitoring criteria.

### 8.3 When you accept a job

At the moment you accept:
- The full property address is revealed
- Relevant access codes are delivered (e.g. key box combination) — these are sent at acceptance time, not before
- The Partner Brief for your service type is available in the job detail view
- The homeowner is notified that you have accepted

**Access codes are job-specific.** They are not yours to retain or share. Treat them as single-use operational credentials.

## Step 9 — Payment Configuration

**Estimated time:** 5 minutes

### 9.1 How payment works

You invoice homeowners directly. Nauxica does not process payments on your behalf for cleaning, maintenance, or laundry jobs. The invoicing relationship is between you and the homeowner.

**Exception: Experiences and Transfers booked via the AI concierge.** When a guest requests a transfer or experience through the Nauxica AI concierge, Nauxica processes the payment and remits to you, deducting the platform commission. See your Partner Agreement for the commission rate and payment timeline.

### 9.2 Bank account details

Enter your payment details for platform-processed transactions:
1. Navigate to **Profile → Payment settings**
2. Enter your IBAN and account holder name
3. Select currency: EUR

For direct-invoice jobs: invoice the homeowner using your own invoicing process. The platform generates a job record with a reference number — include it on your invoice to help homeowners match payments.

### 9.3 Invoicing notes for Italian VAT

⚠️ **Legal note:** Your invoicing obligations depend on your business structure (individual vs. company, VAT registered or not, regime forfettario, etc.). Nauxica does not provide tax advice. Confirm your invoicing obligations with your commercialista.

If you operate under a Partita IVA with standard VAT (22%), ensure your invoices to homeowners include the correct IVA rate and your Partita IVA number. Nauxica may request a copy of job invoices for platform audit purposes.

## Response Time Commitments

Your service quality on the platform is tracked. Homeowners rate completed jobs. The platform tracks:

| Metric | What it measures |
|---|---|
| Response rate | Percentage of job requests responded to within the response window |
| Acceptance rate | Percentage of responded requests that were accepted |
| Job completion rate | Percentage of accepted jobs completed as described |
| Average homeowner rating | Star rating (1–5) from homeowner post-job reviews |
| Dispute rate | Number of disputes opened in any 90-day period |

There are no minimums at MVP. Monitoring is manual. As volume grows, these metrics will inform automatic risk classification (see [partner-vetting.md](../../trust-safety/partner-vetting.md)).

## Common Application Issues

| Issue | Cause | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| Insurance certificate rejected | Coverage amount not clearly stated; wrong insured entity | Request a new certificate from your insurer stating RC Terzi coverage amount explicitly |
| Criminal record certificate (casellario giudiziale) — where to get it | Not knowing the process | Request via the Ministero della Giustizia online portal or your local Procura. Takes several working days. You request it for yourself — Nauxica cannot request it on your behalf. |
| P.IVA not recognised | Format error; recently issued | Double-check the 11-digit number. Recently issued P.IVA may take a few days to appear on official databases. |
| Profile rejected as too vague | Service description does not specify area or experience | Rewrite with: years of experience, number of properties, specific comune(s) served, type of properties (villas, apartments, etc.) |
| Tourism guide licence uncertainty | Unclear if your activity type requires one | Contact the Ente Nazionale per il Turismo (ENIT) or Regione Siciliana tourism office. Do not register for experiences until you have confirmed your licence status. |
| Operating area too broad | Partner accepts jobs then declines due to distance | Narrow your area to what you can reliably reach. Update at any time — this does not affect past assignments. |

## Ongoing Obligations

You are responsible for maintaining the following throughout your active period:

| Obligation | Frequency | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Keep insurance certificates current | Before expiry | Platform sends 30-day renewal reminder. Lapsed insurance = automatic account pause. |
| Update operating area if coverage changes | As needed | Do not let outdated area settings cause you to receive requests you cannot fulfil |
| Maintain response rate | Per job | Failing to respond within window three times in 90 days triggers a review flag |
| Report issues found at properties | Per job | Use the platform's issue report tool — do not contact homeowners outside the platform for property problems |
| Confirm job completion | Per job | Mark jobs as complete in the platform and upload completion photos where required |
| Update availability for planned absences | Before absence | Use the availability settings to flag periods when you are not available — this prevents requests from being sent when you cannot accept |
