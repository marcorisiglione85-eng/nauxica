# Partner FAQ

This document contains frequently asked questions from service partners using Nauxica to receive, manage and complete operational work for hospitality properties.

---

# About Nauxica

## What is Nauxica?

Nauxica is a hospitality operations platform that connects property owners, guests and service partners through a single operational workspace.

## Who are service partners?

Service partners are businesses or individuals providing services such as cleaning, maintenance, inspections, laundry, guest support and operational assistance.

## Why should I use Nauxica?

Nauxica helps partners receive work requests, manage schedules, communicate with property owners and track completed jobs.

## Is Nauxica free for partners?

Partner access depends on the commercial agreement with Nauxica and property owners.

## Can I manage multiple clients?

Yes. Partners can work across multiple properties and homeowners.

## Can I use Nauxica on mobile?

Yes. Nauxica is designed to work on desktop, tablet and mobile devices.

## Can I receive notifications?

Yes. Nauxica can notify partners about new assignments and updates.
