# Guest FAQ

This document contains general guest questions and answers about using Nauxica before, during and after a stay.

---

# About Nauxica

## What is Nauxica?

Nauxica is a hospitality concierge platform designed to support guests before, during and after their stay. It helps guests access property information, receive recommendations, request assistance and communicate with the property team.

## Is Nauxica available 24/7?

Nauxica Concierge is available at all times to answer common questions and route requests when needed.

## Can I speak with a human?

Yes. If your request cannot be resolved automatically, Nauxica can escalate it to the property owner, manager or support team.

## Which languages does Nauxica support?

Nauxica can assist guests in multiple languages. Availability may vary depending on the property and support team.

## Do I need to install an app?

No. Nauxica works through web links, messaging channels and supported communication tools.

## Is Nauxica free for guests?

Yes. Guests can use Nauxica without additional charges.

## Can I use Nauxica before I arrive?

Yes. Nauxica can provide information and assistance before check-in.

## Can I use Nauxica after check-out?

Yes. Depending on the property, Nauxica may continue assisting with invoices, forgotten items or local information.
