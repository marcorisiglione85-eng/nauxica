# Homeowner FAQ

This document contains frequently asked questions from homeowners using Nauxica to manage their properties, reservations, guests, operations and communications.

---

# About Nauxica

## What is Nauxica?

Nauxica is a hospitality operations and AI concierge platform designed to help property owners manage guest communication, reservations, operations, service partners and property information from a single workspace.

## Who is Nauxica designed for?

Nauxica is designed for short-term rental owners, holiday home operators, serviced accommodation providers and hospitality managers.

## Does Nauxica replace booking platforms?

No. Nauxica complements booking platforms by managing guest experience and property operations.

## Can I manage multiple properties?

Yes. Nauxica is designed to support portfolios ranging from a single property to multiple locations.

## Can I invite team members?

This capability may be available depending on your plan and configuration.

## Can I access Nauxica from mobile devices?

Yes. Nauxica works on desktop, tablet and mobile devices.

## Does Nauxica support multiple languages?

Yes. Nauxica is designed to support multilingual hospitality operations.
