# Concierge FAQ

This document contains frequently asked questions specifically about Nauxica Concierge, the AI hospitality assistant used to support guests before, during and after their stay.

---

# About Nauxica Concierge

## What is Nauxica Concierge?

Nauxica Concierge is an AI-powered hospitality assistant that helps guests access information, receive recommendations, solve common problems and communicate with property teams.

## Is Nauxica Concierge a human?

No. Nauxica Concierge is an artificial intelligence assistant. When needed, it can escalate requests to a human.

## What can Nauxica Concierge help with?

Nauxica Concierge can assist with:

- Check-in information
- Property information
- House rules
- WiFi details
- Local recommendations
- Transport guidance
- Maintenance requests
- Guest support
- Escalations to homeowners or partners

## Is Nauxica Concierge available 24/7?

Yes.

## Can Nauxica Concierge speak different languages?

Yes. Nauxica Concierge can communicate in multiple languages.

## Does Nauxica Concierge know everything about the property?

Only the information provided by the homeowner and operational teams.

## Can Nauxica Concierge make decisions on behalf of the homeowner?

No. Nauxica Concierge provides information and assistance but cannot override homeowner policies.

# Limitations

## What can Nauxica Concierge not do?

Nauxica Concierge cannot:

- Make legal decisions
- Make financial decisions
- Authorise refunds
- Override property rules
- Contact emergency services
- Guarantee third-party services
- Diagnose technical faults with certainty

## Can Nauxica Concierge make mistakes?

Like any AI system, it may occasionally provide incomplete or incorrect information. Important matters should be verified with the property team.

## What should happen if the concierge gives an incorrect answer?

The issue should be reported and escalated for review.

## Is Nauxica Concierge always learning from conversations?

No. Learning and knowledge updates follow controlled processes.

## What makes Nauxica Concierge effective?

Accurate property information, well-maintained FAQs, clear operational procedures and up-to-date homeowner knowledge.
